# Your study space

On an iKnow course, use the bookmark beside a word or sentence to save it.
The practice icon saves and opens that item directly. You can also open
**Study library & speaking practice** from the extension popup. The library
supports search and filters for course and item type. Removing an item affects
this saved library only; it does not erase course-preparation results.

Romaji starts concealed on the study card. Reveal one word or all readings.
The original Japanese, available Kana and saved translations remain visible.

**Listen. Pause. Say it.** repeats the complete original recording with a chosen
pause for you to speak. Repetition count, pause length and original playback
speed are selectable. Stop, changing items or hiding/leaving the page stops the
practice loop. This mode uses the source recording, not chopped sentence words
or a substitute computer voice. Original recordings require their iKnow host
to remain accessible; this feature is not an offline recording archive.

**Listen and compare** asks for microphone access only after Record my voice is
clicked. Record for up to twenty seconds, stop, then compare your take with the
original. Takes stay in this page's memory, are never uploaded or added to the
saved library, and are discarded when changing targets or leaving the page.
Tracks are stopped after recording, cancellation, hiding or navigation, including
a microphone grant that arrives after cancellation. The normal Brave microphone
permission prompt applies; no extra manifest permission is added.

## Pronunciation feedback

This is a listening comparison, **not an automatic pronunciation grade**.
Recognizing the expected words does not establish natural pronunciation,
correct vowel length or Japanese pitch accent. The existing Whisper, Qwen and
Aivis components have not been validated as an assessment system. No numerical
score or Good/Medium/Bad label is derived from them.

Dedicated Japanese assessment research exists, but a reliable assessment in this
application would need a defined scope and independent validation against human
judgements. It must be able to abstain when uncertain. See the official
[Whisper model card](https://github.com/openai/whisper/blob/main/model-card.md)
and this [Japanese pitch-accent assessment study](https://www.isca-archive.org/interspeech_2024/masudakatsuse24_interspeech.pdf).
