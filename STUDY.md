# Your study space

Open **Study library & speaking practice** from the extension popup. The sidebar
has Study, Practice, Test and Kana. Use **Library** at the bottom to show or hide
your saved entries.

## Study

Bookmark words and sentences on an iKnow course, or use its practice icon to
save an entry and open it in Study. New saves appear below existing entries.
Search or filter by course and type. Selecting an entry shows its Japanese,
Kana, Romaji and the chosen translation language. Reveal Romaji all at once
or word by word. The course title opens that entry's original course.

Notes appear below the meaning. Edit and save them explicitly; unsaved note
drafts remain available while this page is open. Editing a note does not move
the entry in your library. Delete removes only the saved entry, not prepared
course translations, colors or audio timings. Delete all asks for confirmation.

**Backup** exports or restores saved entries, notes and learning progress.
Import merges entries and shows a summary before confirmation. Backups do not
contain models, recorded takes or downloaded voice files. The library supports
up to 2,000 saved entries.

## Practice

Choose **Saved library** and select the words and sentences you want to use,
or choose **Course** and load an iKnow course ID or link. Course practice uses
the full course, independently of your bookmarks. Choose vocabulary, sentences
or both, then use library/course order or Shuffle.

- **Review:** choose the matching meaning.
- **Fill the gap:** type the missing word. Choose a random suitable word or
  the first suitable word; a vocabulary entry tests that word in its context.
- **Listening:** play the original recording and type what you heard.

Listening and gaps accept the reliable Japanese, Kana or Romaji readings
provided by the source. Whitespace, punctuation and width differences do not
need to match; meaningful long vowels and consonant doubling still matter.
Exercises without enough reliable source data cannot be selected, with the
reason shown rather than silently skipping them.

**One attempt** reveals the answer after one submission. **One retry** allows
one further submission after an incorrect answer, without giving spelling
hints. A correct answer after a retry is recorded separately from a correct
first answer. Show answer counts as an unsuccessful answer.

Refreshing an active round returns to its current exercise and retained
answer state. Audio never restarts automatically. **End session** opens the
results; unanswered questions remain unanswered.

## Course tests and results

Test loads a complete course by ID or link. Choose vocabulary, sentences or
both. Tests use course order and one attempt; they are not limited to saved
entries. Ending early still shows the complete report.

Results show correct first attempts, incorrect answers, answers completed
after a retry and unanswered questions. The percentage is based on correct
first attempts. Select an answer to compare your submission with the expected
answer. Large reports show 50 answers per page and include search plus
**All answers / To review** arrow controls. Practising mistakes preserves the
original test report. Finished results can also be left through the sidebar.

Practice history supplies New, Learning, Weak and Strong library labels. These
describe local practice results, not certified language proficiency. Missed
entries receive an earlier next review; this does not force them into every
manually selected practice round.

## Hiragana and Katakana

The chart shows basic, voiced and combination groups together. Choose groups
or individual characters to practise, then Shuffle or Chart order. Select all
and Deselect all affect the active groups; All groups and Deselect groups
control which groups are included. Choices survive a refresh. Compact is the
default; Large changes only the chart, not exercises or results.

## Audio and recording

**Listen to original** uses the iKnow recording. **Listen & compare** lets you
record up to twenty seconds and play your take alongside the original. Takes
stay in this tab's memory and are never uploaded or saved in a backup.
Microphone access starts only when you click Record my voice, and stops after
recording, cancellation, hiding or leaving the page. Changing the selected
entry discards its take. This is listening comparison, without an automatic
pronunciation grade.

**Volume** in Study adjusts original audio and playback of your recording.
Listening exercises show only original audio volume. The popup also has a
separate Word voice level for the local Mao Plain / Nise voices used when
clicking words on iKnow. Those generated word voices are not used in Study.
Playback levels are saved and can change during playback; they do not change
microphone gain, audio files or voice quality. Original iKnow recordings still
need to be reachable online.

## Themes and readings

Original, Midnight, Tokyo Neon and Kyoto Forest apply to extension pages and
the popup. The theme chosen in the iKnow toolbar controls only the course and
series panels. Source spelling keeps iKnow's Romaji; Pronunciation changes
confirmed sentence particles to wa/e/o. Saved source text remains unchanged.

Refresh retains the current Study entry, filters, Practice course setup and
Kana choices. Settings, models and prepared courses are preserved by signed
updates installed at the existing application folder.
