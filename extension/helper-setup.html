Set up once.
Keep learning.

One setup menu for installation and repair.

  1. 1

    Load the extension first

    In brave://extensions, enable Developer mode and load the unpacked extension folder. Then open the application's helper folder.

  2. 2

    Open Setup.cmd

    Choose 1 — Complete for course preparation and both voices (about 8 GB), or 2 — Voices only (about 2 GB). For an existing or interrupted installation, choose 3 — Check / repair; it repairs only the capabilities already installed or previously selected. 0 exits.

  3. 3

    Return to your course

    Reload the extension and course page. For word voices, enable Click words to listen, then choose Female or Male under Voice.

Wait for Setup complete. and Created by ilyasnsir1. No shell commands are needed. Check / repair does not add course models to a voices-only setup; on a fresh PC, choose 1 or 2 first. Romaji and original iKnow recordings work without model setup. Saved course results are retained.
